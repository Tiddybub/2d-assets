== The Cyberwand ==

The cyberwand is a standard hacker's device to [do stuff]. It comes in three
standard issues: lvl1, lvl2, lvl3. Hackers have illegally bypassed safety
precautions to enhance lvl3 via chip tuning and call it "lvl4". Megacorp
laboratories have produced a further enhanced but yet untested version 5,
informally called "Cybermorph". Rumors about a legendary unknown hacker who
accidentally created the wand during the process of de-programming a FORTUNE50
corporation's entire intranet are being spread to lure professional
underground hackers to test the Cybermorph wand.

== Art history ==

Made after kiba mentioned "cyberpunk" without any artistical guidance
at <http://forum.freegamedev.net/viewtopic.php?f=22&t=307> and trying to test
Decker <http://sourceforge.net/projects/decker/> (recording a video in the
process <http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZW2MVZYvhT4>).
